The default source of fund is set once, on the first checkout. After that, Summary pays with it and Change switches for one order.

UX changes vs. old flow

  • First checkout only: a one-time setup screen. Nothing is preselected, and continuing without a choice shows an error.
  • With a default set, Summary shows "Pay with…" and continues with it. No extra screen.
  • Change on Summary switches this order only. "Save as my default" makes it stick.
  • Credit line goes straight to review. Down payment and payment plan only appear for loans.
  • A toast confirms when a source of fund is activated or saved as the default.
