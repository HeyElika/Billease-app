Do

  • +Stick to three weights: Regular 400 for body copy, SemiBold 600 for emphasis and buttons, Bold 700 for headings. These cover almost everything.

Don't

  • −Don't mix Overused Grotesk with Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, or Source Sans Pro in the same asset.
  • −Don't set body copy in all caps. Caps are for small labels only, with letter-spacing added.
  • −Don't use Light 300 or Book 350. They are in the family but not in our set.
  • −Don't fake a weight by adding a stroke. Use the real weight from the family.

03

Interface icons

Interface icons come from the Solar pack, in two styles only: Outline and Bold. These are the small functional icons such as settings, calendar, and notifications. They are never used to represent a product.

Outline

Default state

Bold

Active or selected state

Rules

  • +Solar Outline and Bold only. The pack also ships Linear, Broken, Line Duotone, and Bold Duotone. We do not use those.
  • +Outline is the default. Bold is for the active or selected state, the same way the app pairs outline and fill icons.
  • +Pick one style per asset. Do not put an Outline icon next to a Bold one unless one of them is genuinely active.
  • +Icons take one flat color from the palette, usually neutral 900 #1A1A1A or white on dark.
  • +Size in even steps: 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48. Scale the whole icon, never redraw it.

Never

  • −Never mix in Font Awesome, Material, Heroicons, Lucide, or a stock icon you found.
  • −Never use an emoji in place of an icon.
  • −Never add a gradient, drop shadow, or extra stroke to a UI icon.
  • −Never use a UI icon for a product. Products get the 3D set below.
Browse and download Solar on Streamline The whole pack. Switch the style to Outline or Bold, then download the SVG or PNG.
04

Product icons

Every Billease product has one 3D icon. These are the only images allowed to stand in for a product. If you need a product icon that is not here, ask design. Do not source a lookalike.

Rules

  • +Always on a plain background: white or neutral 100 #F5F5F5.
  • +Download the PNG from the shared drive. Never screenshot one or pull it out of a deck.
  • +Leave clear space of at least 25% of the icon width on every side.
  • +When several appear together, size them to the same bounding box so they read as one family.

Never

  • −Never recolor, flatten, or add an outline. The colors are part of the product identity.
  • −Never rotate or add a second shadow.
  • −Never place one over a busy photo. They lose their edges.
  • −Never mix them with a flat or line icon in the same lockup.
  • −Never substitute a 3D icon from another pack. The set is closed.
